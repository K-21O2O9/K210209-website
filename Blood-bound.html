Tactical Command - Game Concepts

Victory Condition

Each player controls an army featuring 1 Commander and 8 tactical units. Defeat the opponent's Commander to immediately win the match. Protect your Commander at all costs!

Unit Classes & Roster

COMMANDER (x1)

100 HP | High attack. Loss of Commander ends the game immediately.

KNIGHT (x2)

40 HP | Frontline tank with heavy melee damage.

ARCHER (x2)

22 HP | Long attack range (3 tiles). Fragile in close combat.

MAGE (x2)

18 HP | Mid range (2 tiles). High burst tactical damage.

SCOUT (x2)

25 HP | Exceptional movement range (4 tiles). Ideal for flank attacks.

Turn & Movement Controls

  • Your Turn: You can only select and order your own colored units (Blue = P1, Red = P2).
  • Movement: Click your unit to view blue highlighted valid movement tiles. Click a highlighted tile to move.
  • Combat: Enemy units in red highlighted tiles can be targeted. Click an enemy in range to attack!
  • End Turn: Click the "End Turn" button when finished to pass control to your opponent.
