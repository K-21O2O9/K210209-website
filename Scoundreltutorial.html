The Cards of Fate

The dungeon deck is forged from three suits, each bearing a rank from 2 through Ace (Ace = 14). Every card is a choice — every choice, a scar.

♠ Monster
♣ Monster
♦ Weapon
♥ Potion
  • ♠ ♣ Monsters — Deal wounds equal to their rank. They must be slain or evaded.
  • ♦ Weapons — Reduce incoming damage. Each strike leaves a scar on the blade — a durability limit.
  • ♥ Potions — Restore vitality by their rank. Only one draught per room holds power; further swigs are worthless.
⚈

The Chamber of Souls

Each chamber reveals four face-down cards on the cold stone. You must resolve exactly three before the passage forward opens. The fourth is abandoned to the darkness — forever lost.

Whisper of caution: Survey all four cards before acting. If a towering horror lurks among them, consider fleeing the entire chamber — but know this: you may only flee before resolving a single card, and only once per room.
⚔

The Art of Slaughter

When you grip a weapon, its rank deflects the monster's fury. The calculus of blood is simple:

Wounds Sustained = Monster Rank − Weapon Rank  (never below zero)

After the kill, the weapon's durability is branded with the monster's rank. Henceforth, the blade may only be wielded against foes whose rank is strictly lower than that threshold.

  • Example: You bear a 7♦ blade. A 10♠ horror attacks — you suffer 3 wounds, and the blade's durability becomes 10. Now it serves only against monsters ranked 9 or below.

Without a weapon — or when your blade has grown too dull — you must face the monster barehanded, enduring the full brunt of its rank in damage.

☤

Elixirs of the Flesh

Potions knit wounds by their rank, but the body grows tolerant. Only the first potion in any chamber mends flesh; all others are bitter water. Your mortal shell caps at 20 vitality — do not squander healing.

⏲

The Doom Clock

The dungeon grants sixty heartbeats per seven-floor cycle. When the last grain falls, your soul is forfeit — instant death. Survive seven chambers, and the sands turn anew. Speed is survival. Hesitation is the grave.

★

The Tally of Triumph

Each floor cleared brands +1 mark upon your legacy. The dungeon yields only when every last monster in the entire deck lies dead at your feet. Your greatest score is etched into the stone — can you surpass it?

☠

The End of the Road

The darkness claims you when:

  • Your vitality drains to zero
  • The Doom Clock expires

Flee with wisdom. Once a chamber's first card is resolved, the door seals behind you — no escape until three are dealt with, or the room is cleared.
